How to use this CGPA Calculator

  1. Enter the course name, credit unit, and grade point.
  2. Click "Add Course" to save it.
  3. The course list will appear below, showing all your entries.
  4. Click "Delete" next to a course to remove it.
  5. Click "Clear All Courses" to remove all courses at once.
  6. Your courses are saved automatically and will stay even if you close the page.
  7. Click "Calculate CGPA" to see your current CGPA.
